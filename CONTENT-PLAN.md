# Content plan

How to produce the content that makes the SEO that is already built actually
rank. The technical work is done; this is the part that earns visibility.

---

## Where you stand

The site currently has **four pages**: a homepage and three properties. That is
a shop with four items in the window. Google ranks *pages*, not sites, so with
four pages there are only four chances to match a search — and none of them
target the phrases people actually type.

The good news is that you are not competing for "apartment Accra" against
everyone. You have something most competitors don't: **three specific
properties in three specific places.** Specific beats general.

---

## The vocabulary that matters

Research on the Ghanaian market shows the trade talks about **"short let"** and
**"short stay"**, not "vacation rental" or "holiday home". Meanwhile guests
search by area — East Legon, Airport Residential, Adenta, Aburi.

So the phrases to build around are combinations of:

- **Intent:** short let · short stay · furnished apartment · accommodation · where to stay · Airbnb
- **Place:** Adenta · Aburi · Lakeside Estate · Greater Accra · Eastern Region

> ⚠️ **Check volumes before you commit.** I have not paid for keyword data, so
> the topics below are chosen by *intent* — what a person typing this needs —
> not by measured volume. Put the seed phrases into Google Keyword Planner
> (free), Ahrefs, or Ubersuggest and let the numbers reorder the list. Write the
> ones that come back with real volume first.

## The three content types

These are ranked by return on effort. Types 1 and 2 do almost all the work.

### Type 1 — Location guides *(highest return)*

One page per place you operate. These capture people who know *where* they want
to stay but not *who* to book with — the largest and most convertible group.

Every guide should answer, honestly and in this order:

1. What the area is actually like (who it suits, who it doesn't)
2. How to get there, and from where (airport, city centre, nearest landmark)
3. What's nearby — food, shops, attractions, with rough travel times
4. Best time to visit, and what the weather does
5. Where to stay, mentioning your property naturally as one option

### Type 2 — Experience and occasion pages

People don't search for "apartment"; they search for the occasion. You already
offer honeymoon, birthday and car-rental packages — each deserves a page.

### Type 3 — Practical answers

The questions guests ask before booking. You already have five in the FAQ; each
one is a page, and each one can win a featured snippet.

## The plan — twelve pages

Ordered as I would write them. Each entry gives the target, the intent, and an
outline.

### 1. `Where to stay in Accra: a neighbourhood guide`
**Target:** where to stay in Accra · best area to stay in Accra
**Intent:** early research — the reader is deciding *where*, not yet *who*.
**Outline:** East Legon · Airport Residential · Osu · Adenta · Lakeside Estate ·
what each costs · safety and traffic notes · who each suits. End with a short
comparison table. Mention your Adenta and Lakeside properties once each, as
examples — not as the point of the page.

### 2. `Short let apartments in Adenta: what to expect`
**Target:** short let Adenta · short stay Adenta · furnished apartment Adenta
**Intent:** transactional — ready to book.
**Outline:** what a short let includes in Ghana · typical pricing bands ·
deposit and ID expectations · what's around Adenta · your Adenta property with
photos, capacity and a booking link.

### 3. `Staying in Aburi: the complete guide`
**Target:** Aburi accommodation · hotels in Aburi · places to stay Aburi ·
things to do in Aburi
**Intent:** mixed — a genuine tourist destination.
**Outline:** the drive up from Accra and how long it really takes · the
botanical gardens · the craft market · weather and the famous cool air · where
to eat · weekend vs midweek · your Aburi Mountain Retreat.

### 4. `Honeymoon in Ghana: where to go and where to stay`
**Target:** honeymoon in Ghana · romantic getaway Ghana · honeymoon apartment Accra
**Intent:** planning, high value, low competition.
**Outline:** why Ghana over the usual options · best regions by season ·
what a honeymoon package typically includes · privacy and what to ask for ·
your honeymoon package.

### 5. `Lakeside Estate, Accra: a local's guide`
**Target:** Lakeside Estate Accra · Lakeside Estate apartment
**Intent:** specific, low volume, very high conversion.
**Outline:** the estate itself · security and gated access · distance to the
airport and to central Accra · shopping · the pool and gym · your Lakeside
property.

### 6. `Furnished vs unfurnished: what a short let actually includes in Ghana`
**Target:** what is included in a short let · furnished apartment Ghana
**Intent:** informational, removes a real hesitation.
**Outline:** line by line — power and ECG/dumsor · water · internet · cleaning
frequency · what guests are expected to bring · typical house rules. This one
converts because it removes doubt.

### 7. `The best time to visit Ghana`
**Target:** best time to visit Ghana · Ghana weather by month
**Intent:** top of funnel, wide reach, feeds every other page.
**Outline:** harmattan vs rainy season · the two rainy peaks · which months
suit the coast vs the hills (Aburi is genuinely better in the hot months) ·
public holidays to avoid or book around.

### 8. `Apartment or hotel in Accra? An honest comparison`
**Target:** apartment vs hotel Accra · serviced apartment Accra
**Intent:** comparison — a decision point.
**Outline:** cost at 1, 3 and 7 nights · kitchens and food costs · space and
privacy · service trade-offs · when a hotel genuinely wins. Be fair here; the
honesty is what earns the share and the link.

### 9. `Pet-friendly accommodation in Accra and Aburi`
**Target:** pet friendly accommodation Ghana · pet friendly short let Accra
**Intent:** narrow, underserved, almost no competition.
**Outline:** what to check before booking with a pet · cleaning fees · your
Aburi property, which already accepts pets.

### 10. `Booking an apartment in Ghana: deposits, ID and payment`
**Target:** how to book an apartment in Ghana · short let deposit
**Intent:** trust — this is where scams make people cautious.
**Outline:** why a legitimate operator asks for ID · how deposits are held and
returned · paying by card and mobile money, and why you should be wary of
transfer-only listings. Link to your own secure Paystack checkout as the
worked example.

### 11. `Things to do in Accra in three days`
**Target:** things to do in Accra · Accra itinerary
**Intent:** high volume, top of funnel. Ranks slowly but feeds everything else.
**Outline:** a real day-by-day itinerary with travel times. End with one line
pointing at your apartments.

### 12. `Long stays in Accra: what monthly rates include`
**Target:** monthly apartment Accra · long stay Accra
**Intent:** high-value, longer bookings, less seasonality.
**Outline:** how monthly rates are priced · utilities and who pays ·
housekeeping · co-working and internet reliability · your monthly rate.

## How to write these so they rank

**Lead with the answer.** First paragraph, plain language. Search engines and
AI assistants both lift the first clear answer they find.

**Write to one question per page.** If a page answers two questions, split it.

**Be specific and local.** "Eight minutes' drive from the Aburi turn-off" beats
"conveniently located". Specificity is what a generic competitor cannot copy.

**Use the real words.** Write "short let", not "vacation rental" — match how
your market speaks.

**Structure with headings.** One `h1`, then `h2` per section, `h3` beneath. The
technical side already supports this.

**Link between pages.** Every guide should link to at least two others and to a
property. Internal links are how a crawler finds new pages and how authority
spreads.

**Photograph it yourself.** Original photos of the actual places outrank stock
photography every time, and you have the properties.

**Avoid:** keyword stuffing, AI-generated filler that says nothing, and copied
descriptions from other listings. All three are penalised now.

## A realistic timeline

Nothing here ranks in a week. Expect:

- **Weeks 1–4:** pages indexed, little traffic. This is normal, not failure.
- **Months 2–3:** long-tail phrases start landing — "short let Adenta" before
  "apartment Accra".
- **Months 4–6:** the guides begin to compound as they gather internal links.
- **Months 6+:** competitive head terms become reachable, if you keep publishing.

Two things that shorten this more than any extra page:

1. **Google Business Profile** — for a local rental business this alone can
   out-perform the website for "near me" searches. Free, and claimable today.
2. **Real guest reviews** — once you have them, we can add `aggregateRating` to
   the structured data, which puts stars in results. I deliberately left it out
   because inventing reviews breaches Google's guidelines.
